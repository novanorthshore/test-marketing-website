Nova Finale: 001

Car Submissions

This is a curated field. We take cars that hold the standard of the day: rare, finished, or built with enough intent that they belong next to the rest of the lineup.

Filling out the form is the start. Acceptance does not reserve a spot. Only payment after review confirms you for Nova Finale 001.

Supercars, rare classics, JDM icons, Euro, modified, and preserved. If you are selling, use Marketplace. If you want to be close without taking a feature stall, use VIP parking.

Apply Now

The 3-step submission process

1. Apply. Complete the form with contact info, vehicle details, and photos where required.

2. Review. We look at the car, the photos, and whether it fits the field. That usually takes a couple of days.

3. Confirm and register. If we take the car, you get an email with a payment link. Pay to lock the spot. No payment, no stall.

VIP parking entry

A dedicated enthusiast paddock closer to the main show, not in the primary display lineup. You still bring a car. You do not get a feature stall.

  • VIP zone vehicle entry
  • Placement next to the curated show area
  • Easy access in and out of the paddock

$10 after approval.
