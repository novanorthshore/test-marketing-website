Nova Finale: 001

Welcome to NOVA Finale:001.

If you’d like your vehicle to be part of our featured show lineup, please take a moment to read through the submission details and complete the application below.

Vancouver’s automotive culture is defined by its incredible diversity, and we’re bringing it all together. From supercars and rare classics to JDM icons, European performance cars, American muscle, modified builds, and standout enthusiast vehicles of every style.

If you’ve joined us before, you already know what NOVA is about. If this is your first event, please upload a few clear photos and share some details about your car so our team can get to know the build.

You don’t need the rarest or most expensive car to apply. We’re looking for vehicles with character, quality, and a story, cars their owners are genuinely proud to share.

Accepted applicants will receive registration and event details by email.

Please Note: Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance, but every vehicle will be given proper consideration.

Apply Now

Different styles, one shared passion for cars

3 Step Submission Process

1. Apply: Complete the vehicle submission form with your contact information, vehicle details, modifications, and clear photos. The more complete your submission is, the easier it is for our team to review.

2. Review: Every vehicle will be individually reviewed by the NOVA team based on its quality, character, presentation, and fit within the overall show. Our goal is to create a carefully balanced lineup representing the full range of Vancouver’s automotive culture, from supercars and classics to JDM, European, modified, and standout enthusiast builds. Please allow our team some time to give every submission the consideration it deserves.

3. Confirm & Register: If your vehicle is accepted, you’ll receive an email with instructions to confirm your spot and complete payment for your show-car registration. Please keep an eye on your inbox and spam folder.
