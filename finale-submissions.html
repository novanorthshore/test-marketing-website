Nova Finale: 001

Car Submissions

If you’d like your vehicle to be considered for our curated show lineup, please review the submission details and complete the application below.

We’re looking for a diverse mix of supercars, rare classics, JDM icons, European performance cars, modern exotics, modified builds, and standout enthusiast vehicles. Please take a few minutes to upload clear photos and provide accurate details about your car so our team can review each submission properly.

Accepted vehicles will be selected based on quality, uniqueness, presentation, and overall fit within the show. Our goal is to build a balanced lineup that reflects the best of Vancouver’s automotive culture.

Please note: submitting your vehicle does not guarantee acceptance. Approved applicants will receive registration and event details by email.

Apply Now

The 3-step submission process

1. Apply: Complete the vehicle submission form with your contact information, vehicle details, modifications, and clear photos. The more complete your submission is, the easier it is for our team to review.

2. Review: The Nova team will review each vehicle individually based on quality, uniqueness, presentation, and overall fit within the event lineup. We’re building a balanced mix of supercars, classics, JDM, Euro, modified, and standout enthusiast vehicles, so please allow time for the review process.

3. Confirm & Register: If your vehicle is accepted, you’ll receive an email with instructions to confirm your spot and complete payment for your show-car registration. Please keep an eye on your inbox and spam folder.

Important: Submitting your vehicle does not guarantee acceptance and does not reserve a spot. Only approved vehicles that complete registration and payment will be confirmed for NOVA FINALE 001.
