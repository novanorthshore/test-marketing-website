Cypress Event

Nova presents the Cypress Mountain Charity Event!

You and your vehicle have been chosen to be a part of a unique lineup of beautiful cars on the peak of Vancouver's Cypress Mountain.

Huge thank you to Cypress Mountain and BC Parks for allowing us to host this event to raise money for North Shore Rescue.

Saturday, May 23, 2026 | 4-7 PM

Attendees are expected to stay for the full length of the event.

There will be zero tolerance for revving, burnouts, or any similar activities. There will be police presence on-site.

Parking Instructions

Only park in the approved lot. Follow the red route. Do not park in the yellow-marked area.

This is the only area we have permission to use. Parking anywhere else puts Nova North Shore's relationship with Cypress Mountain at risk.

Payment is required to confirm your RSVP. Choose a $10 standard spot or a $30 spot that includes professional photography on-site. Each attendee must RSVP separately.
