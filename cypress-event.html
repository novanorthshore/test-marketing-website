Nova Charity Collaboration

Cypress Event

Nova presents the Cypress community charity event! We are collaborating with West Coast Dream Machines, Leah's Automotive, Streetlight Productions, and VanPro Detailing along with Cypress mountain to raise money for North Shore Rescue. This is an invite-only meet so please do not share the details with anybody. We hope to see you there!

A $10 charge per vehicle registered is required, there is also a $30 option that includes photos taken by Nova photographers and all proceeds will be donated to North Shore Rescue
