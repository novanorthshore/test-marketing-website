Block Party Car Show

The Nova North Shore Block Party Car Show is coming to the North Shore on Sunday, July 19, 2026 and we want your car on the street.

We are closing down Lloyd Avenue in North Vancouver for a block party celebrating classic, vintage, American muscle, and European cars. This show is fully permitted by the District of North Vancouver, with judged categories, trophies, and a dedicated dash plaque for every registered car.

Sunday, July 19, 2026 | 3 PM - 7 PM Car check-in at 2 PM on Lloyd Avenue, North Vancouver.

On top of the show we are bringing a live DJ, local food vendors, and a full evening of community and culture in the heart of North Vancouver.

Spots are limited to 150 cars and this is a curated show. All applications are reviewed to make sure every car on Lloyd Avenue belongs there: classic builds, full restorations, vintage muscle, and European icons only.

Apply below to secure a spot. Approved applicants will receive a confirmation email with payment instructions within 48 hours.
