Built By The Community

Nova North Shore started in 2022 as a small gathering of committed automotive enthusiasts and has grown into one of the coast's most active car communities. The club welcomes everything from lifted trucks and timeless classics to slammed JDM, Euro builds, motorcycles, exotics, muscle cars, and custom projects.

Every meet is designed to bring drivers, photographers, shops, sponsors, and local businesses together around safe, respectful, high-quality motorsport culture.

Past Collaborations

Nova X BC Lions

In 2025, Nova collaborated with the BC Lions to plan and market their official Show & Shine event, bringing fans, car enthusiasts, and local businesses together.

Nova X Midas North Vancouver

Nova teamed up with Midas North Vancouver to host a Euro vs. JDM event with a diverse lineup, packed crowd, and a showcase of style and performance.

Nova X Benzo Cafe

Nova joined forces with Benzo Cafe for a Cars & Coffee event that blended community vibes, diverse builds, shared passion, and great coffee.

Nova X 2JD Diecast

Nova partnered with 2JD Diecast, a local diecast distributor, to bring unique collectibles and giveaways that added extra excitement for enthusiasts of all ages.

Nova X GoodCo Bars

Nova teamed up with GoodCo Bars to promote their F1 watch party, driving turnout and creating an unforgettable motorsport experience off the track.

Nova X That's Good Ice Cream

Nova partnered with That's Good Ice Cream to bring sweet treats to car events, adding a fun and tasty element that made meets more memorable.

Nova X Vancouver Honda Club

Nova teamed up with the Vancouver Honda Club to celebrate Honda builds, bring enthusiasts together, and strengthen local car culture in Vancouver.

Nova X UBC Sports Car Club

Nova partnered with the UBC Sports Car Club to unite student and local car communities, showcase diverse builds, and foster new connections.

Nova X Lower Mainland Euros

Nova partnered with Lower Mainland Euros to celebrate Euro builds, bring communities together, and create unforgettable experiences across the Lower Mainland.

Nova X BC Beamers

Nova teamed up with BC Beamers to celebrate BMW builds, bring together the local Euro car community, and highlight standout vehicles.

Nova X HanzDriven

Nova connected with HanzDriven to support local automotive culture through community presence, event energy, and enthusiast-focused coverage.

Nova X Drive Motorsports

Nova worked with Drive Motorsports as an automotive partner supporting performance-minded enthusiasts, clean builds, and local event culture.

Nova X Thinkware

Nova partnered with Thinkware to connect the community with practical automotive technology and support a stronger event experience.

Nova X Diamond Auto

Partnering with Diamond Auto Repair, Nova hosted a JDM-themed meet with clean builds, live DJ sets, local sponsor support, and a strong North Shore turnout.

Nova X Cru App

Nova partnered with the Cru app to help enthusiasts discover local meets, showcase builds, follow events, and connect with like-minded car lovers.

Nova X Spots Garage

Nova teamed up with Spots Garage, a local automotive apparel brand, to bring merchandise, event support, and authentic car-culture style to the community.

Nova X Driving Unicorns

Nova collaborated with Driving Unicorns, creators of automotive-inspired artwork and apparel, to bring unique designs and creative energy to events.
