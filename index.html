Built By The Community.

Nova North Shore started in 2022 as a small gathering of friends who shared a passion for cars. Being far from most of Greater Vancouver's large car events, we set out to build something closer to home.

Since then, we've grown into one of Vancouver's most active car communities with a mission to change what car culture looks like in Vancouver. We believe this community deserves better than unsanctioned parking lot meets and takeovers. Every Nova event is permitted and insured, backed by sponsors on-site — proof that car culture can be done legit.

From timeless classics to modern supercars, slammed JDMs to race-built Euros, Nova welcomes every corner of the scene. Every meet is built to bring drivers, photographers, shops, sponsors, and local businesses together around safe, respectful, high-quality motorsport culture.
