Current Event

Motors & Matcha

Join us for a open house event at Mike's Auto and Whisk & Wrench Matcha! Nova North Shore invites you to Motors & Matcha, a relaxed morning meet hosted at Mike's Auto, a Porsche-certified mechanic shop in Richmond. Browse the bays, talk and build stories with fellow enthusiasts, and fuel up on matcha from Whisk & Wrench — conveniently located right above the shop. Whether you're rolling in on four wheels or just coming to soak it all in, this is a morning worth showing up for.
