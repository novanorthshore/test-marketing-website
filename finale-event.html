Plaza of Nations, Vancouver, BC

Nova Finale: 001

September 13, 2026

Nova Finale is the close of the year for this community. One day at Plaza of Nations. A curated field of cars, a marketplace for cars that are actually for sale, and a paddock for people who want to be in it without taking a feature spot.

We are looking for supercars, classics, JDM, Euro, modified, and preserved builds. If the car has a story and it is finished enough to stand still for a full afternoon, we want to see it.

Spectators get in free. Feature cars, marketplace listings, and VIP parking go through the application. Nothing is confirmed until we review it and you pay.

Submit an application

Car Submissions

Supercars. Classics. JDM. Euro. Modified. Preserved.

Show-car spots are reviewed. Apply with photos and vehicle details. If we take the car, you get an email with a payment link to lock the spot.

Event FAQ

Nova Finale: 001 time, date, location?

Saturday, September 13, 2026 at Plaza of Nations, Vancouver, BC. Load-in and show hours go out in the confirmation email after your application is accepted.

Is the event free for spectators?

Yes. Walking in to look at cars is free. Feature display, marketplace listings, and VIP parking are paid after approval.

Can I enter my car?

Apply through car submissions. Choose featured show car, Nova Marketplace, or VIP parking. We review every application. Acceptance is not a reserved spot until you pay.

What is the Nova Marketplace?

A for-sale lane for enthusiast-owned and dealership cars. Accepted listings get a display plaque with a QR code so people can pull details on the car without hovering at the windshield.

Is it rain or shine?

The show is planned rain or shine. If weather forces a move, we will post the rain date on this page and email registered cars.
