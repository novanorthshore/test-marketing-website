NOVA FINALE 001 is the beginning of a new annual automotive tradition in Vancouver, a curated celebration of cars, design, culture, and community.

Set at Plaza of Nations, the event will bring together supercars, rare classics, JDM icons, European performance cars, modern exotics, and standout enthusiast builds from across the Lower Mainland.

More than a traditional car show, FINALE 001 is designed as an experience, featuring vehicle reveals, brand showcases, the NOVA Marketplace, vendors, music, food, awards, and interactive activations throughout the afternoon.

Every element is intentionally selected to create a show people look forward to year after year.

Plaza of Nations venue

Set along Vancouver's False Creek waterfront, Plaza of Nations provides one of the city's most iconic settings for NOVA FINALE: 001. Located beside BC Place and surrounded by the downtown skyline, the open-air venue combines waterfront views, modern city architecture, and plenty of space for an unforgettable automotive showcase.

Its central location makes it easily accessible from across the Lower Mainland, creating the perfect place to bring together standout cars, local businesses, and Vancouver's automotive community.

Is the event free for spectators?

Yes. Nova Finale 001 is free for spectators. No general admission ticket is required to attend and enjoy the show. Show-car registration, Nova Marketplace listings, and certain premium experiences have separate fees.

Can I bring my dog?

The Plaza of Nations offers ample space for dogs to walk and explore, meters away from Coopers Dog Park. Please bring a leash, and clean up after them.

Can I enter my car?

Yes. Nova Finale 001 is a curated show, so all vehicles must be submitted for review before being accepted. We welcome supercars, classics, JDM, European performance, modified builds, rare vehicles, and other standout enthusiast cars.

Is it rain or shine?

FINALE 001 is scheduled for September 13. In the event of severe weather, the event may be postponed to our rain date of September 20. All registrations and tickets will automatically transfer to the rain date. Any changes will be announced through our website, email, and social media.
